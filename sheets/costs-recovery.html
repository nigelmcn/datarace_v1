THE RECONCILIATION LOGIC

INPUTS

Costs

Accommodation, transport and services, from accounting

Assignments

Which worker was in which room, each day

Deductions

What was taken from each worker’s pay

STEP 1 · ALLOCATE

Share each cost across the workers assigned on the days it applies

STEP 2 · COMPARE

For each worker: balance = deducted − allocated cost

OUTCOME PER WORKER

Balance below zero

Under-recovered → Clawback

Balance is zero

Fully recovered → Reconciled

Balance above zero

Over-deducted → Refund
