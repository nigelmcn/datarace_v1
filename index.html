The problem

Your records are spread across four systems, and the Department wants answers from all of them.

Most Approved Employers run on a recruitment platform, a payroll system, an accounting package and a stack of spreadsheets. Each holds part of the record for a worker. None holds all of it.

That works until a question crosses systems. A worker disputes a rent deduction. A compliance officer asks how transport costs for a site were worked out. A client wants to know why their margin dropped. Someone spends days pulling exports into a spreadsheet, and the answer is only as good as their copy and paste.

The PALM rules make this harder than ordinary labour hire. Under the Approved Employer Guidelines:

  • Deductions must be explained, agreed in writing before they're made, principally for the worker's benefit, and good value.
  • In any week you offer a worker less than 20 hours, you pay their accommodation and transport yourself, and no debt can build up.
  • Each worker keeps at least $200 a week after tax and deductions.
  • Pay, deductions and net financial benefit sit first on the Department's assurance priorities for 2026–27.

I've had to answer these questions at Agri-Labour Australia, for 2,000 people. The solutions below are what I built so those answers come from one place.

Sources: PALM Approved Employer Guidelines v2.1, DEWR Deed and Guidelines settings, PALM employer assurance and compliance.

Where most people start

Do you need to recover accommodation and transport costs from your workers?

Costs come in from accounting and deductions from payroll. You record which worker was in which room and vehicle on each day. The system splits every cost across the people who were actually there and compares it with what was taken from their pay.

At the end of each period every worker has a balance.

Below zeroUnder-recovered. Clawback scheduled.
ZeroFully recovered. Reconciled.
Above zeroOver-deducted. Refund owed.

A late move-out or a changed expense date waits for approval before it counts. Each period is signed off and locked as a PDF pack, so if a worker or the Department asks, you can show what was charged, why, and who approved it.

What I build

Twelve proven solutions to common Approved Employer problems.

Each one is proven in a working Approved Employer, and each can be customised to fit your business exactly. It's built on your own systems and data, and you own the result. Each has a one-page cheat sheet showing how it works.

Pay and compliance records

Can you show every accommodation and transport deduction was fair?

Costs Recovery

Allocates each cost to the workers assigned on the days it applied, compares it with what was deducted, and locks each period as a signed-off pack.

Could you prove every worker's right to work today?

Identity and Working Rights

Collects passport and visa details, checks the visa conditions allow the role, stores the evidence, and flags expiries at 90 and 30 days.

How long would it take to find a worker's current contract?

Contracts and Agreements

Issues agreements from approved templates, records digital signatures, tracks variations, and shows which terms apply to each worker now.

Owning your data

Which of your clients actually make money?

Client Profitability

Combines placements, timesheets and accounting to give gross profit and EBIT for every client and location. The totals add back to your P&L.

If you changed payroll or recruitment vendor, would you lose your history?

Data Warehouse

Extracts data from each system on a schedule into one database you own, matches workers, clients and locations across them, and keeps every change over time.

How much of your field data is still on paper?

Field Data Capture

Phone forms for attendance, incidents and site checks that work without signal, check entries as they're made, and sync into your own data.

Welfare and oversight

When did someone last check in with each of your workers?

Contact Outreach

Schedules welfare check-ins, logs every call, message and visit against the worker, and flags overdue contacts and open concerns.

How many tools have your staff built that IT doesn't know about?

Staff-built App Pathway

A process for staff to register what they've built, have it checked for privacy and security, and get single sign-on, backups and logging before others rely on it.

Training and AI

Can you show what every worker was told at induction, and when?

Instructions and Training

Assigns modules by role, site and language, records acknowledgement, and sends refreshers when rules change.

Are your staff already pasting worker details into free AI tools?

AI Assistant

One approved AI platform connected to your SOPs and data, with a written policy, labelled sensitive data and logged use. Staff draft job orders, summarise reports and message workers in their own language.

When systems fail

Could you pay every worker if payroll went down on pay day?

Payroll Fallback

Calculates pay from the latest timesheets and rates in your own data, gets it approved, pays workers, and reconciles payroll once it's back.

What happens to recruitment if your platform goes down tomorrow?

Staff Portal Fallback

Keeps a current copy of jobs, submissions and placements so recruiters can keep working in a separate portal, then syncs their changes back.
